Please review your documents and let us know right away if anything is incorrect: names, address, drivers, vehicles, coverages or the effective date.

Tell us before these changes happen, so you stay properly covered:

  • Moving to a new address
  • A new driver in your home (including a family member who gets a licence), or a new or replaced vehicle
  • Using your car for delivery, ride-sharing or business
  • Renovations, renting out part of your home, or running a business from home

Leaving out or giving incorrect information can affect a claim, so please keep us updated.
