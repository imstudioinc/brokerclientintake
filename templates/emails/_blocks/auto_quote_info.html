Auto insurance - what we need for your quote

  • Driver's licence: a clear photo of the front of the licence for every licensed driver living in your home, even if they don't drive your car (insurers require us to list them).
  • Vehicle: a photo of the ownership (green permit), or the 17-character VIN if you haven't bought the car yet.
  • Phone number and email address to reach you.

Helpful if you have it (it can lower your price and speeds things up):

  • Your current auto policy's declarations page and renewal date, if you are insured now.
  • How the car is used: pleasure, driving to work or school (approx. km one way), or business, and approx. km per year.
  • Any accidents, claims or tickets in the last 6 years. With your consent we will also check your driving and claims history.
  • If you were licensed outside Ontario: a letter of experience from your previous insurer and your driving record from that country or province.
