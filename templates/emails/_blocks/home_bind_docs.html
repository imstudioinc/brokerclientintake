Home - documents to finalize your policy

  • Void cheque (or a bank direct-deposit form) for monthly payments.
  • Mortgage details: lender's name and address and your mortgage number (the lender is added to the policy).
  • Closing date (if buying) or the date you want the policy to start.
  • Your previous home policy's declarations page, if you were insured before.

Home - please choose your coverage

  • Personal liability limit: $1,000,000 or $2,000,000. We recommend $2,000,000.
  • Deductible: $1,000, $2,500 or another amount.
  • Water coverage - not all water damage is automatically covered. Would you like:
    • Sewer backup (water backing up through drains or toilets)
    • Overland water (flooding from heavy rain or rivers entering at ground level)
  • Any valuable items to list separately (jewellery, watches, art, collections), as these have limits under a standard policy.
