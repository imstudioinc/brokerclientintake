Auto - documents to finalize your policy

  • Void cheque (or a bank direct-deposit form) for monthly payments.
  • Bill of sale, if you recently bought the vehicle.
  • Financing or lease agreement, if the car is financed or leased. We need the lender's name and address on the policy.
  • Winter tires (for the winter tire discount): a photo of one tire showing the mountain and snowflake symbol, with your driver's licence held next to it in the same photo.

Auto - please choose your coverage

  • Liability limit (protects you if you injure someone or damage their property): $1,000,000 or $2,000,000. We recommend $2,000,000.
  • Coverage for your own car:
    • Full coverage - Collision + Comprehensive: pays to repair your car after an at-fault accident, theft, fire, vandalism, hitting an animal, glass damage, etc.; or
    • Liability only (sometimes called "one-way") - no coverage for damage to your own car when you are at fault.
  • Deductible (the amount you pay on a claim for your own car): $500, $1,000 or another amount.
  • Accident benefits (if you or your passengers are injured): medical, rehabilitation and attendant care are included on every policy. Since July 1, 2026 these other benefits are optional and you choose each one:
    • Income replacement (if you can't work after an accident)
    • Non-earner benefit, caregiver benefit, housekeeping and home maintenance
    • Death and funeral benefits
    • Lost educational expenses, expenses of visitors, damage to personal items
    • Higher medical / rehabilitation limits
    We will go over what each one covers with you before you decide. If you choose not to take an optional benefit, we will ask you to confirm that in writing.
