Tenant insurance - what we need for your quote

  • Rental address (including unit number) and your move-in date.
  • Type of rental: apartment / condo building, basement unit, or a whole house.
  • Your belongings: an estimate of what it would cost to replace everything you own (furniture, electronics, clothing, etc.).
  • Who lives with you: family members and any roommates.
  • Anything extra: dogs (breed), or a business run from home.
  • Current policy: if you have tenant insurance now, the declarations page and renewal date.
  • Claims: any home or tenant insurance claims in the last 5 years.
  • Phone number and email address to reach you.
