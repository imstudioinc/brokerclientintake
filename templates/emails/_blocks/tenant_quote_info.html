Tenant insurance - what we need for your quote

  • Rental address (including unit number and postal code) and your move-in date.
  • About each named insured: date of birth, occupation and marital status (needed for the quote and the credit-based pricing).
  • Type of rental: apartment / condo building, basement unit, or a whole house.
  • Your belongings: an estimate of what it would cost to replace everything you own (furniture, electronics, clothing, etc.).
  • Who lives with you: family members and any roommates (and whether they are related to you).
  • Anything extra: dogs (breed), a monitored alarm, or a business run from home.
  • Current policy: if you have tenant insurance now, the declarations page (insurer, policy number and renewal date), and how long you have been continuously insured.
  • Claims: any home or tenant insurance claims in the last 5 years, and whether any insurer has cancelled or declined you.
  • Phone number and email address to reach you.

I will also need your signed consent to check your claims history (HITS) and your credit score - I will send the form.
