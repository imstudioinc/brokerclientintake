Tenant - documents to finalize your policy

  • Void cheque (or a bank direct-deposit form) for monthly payments.
  • The date you want the policy to start (usually your move-in date).

Tenant - please choose your coverage

  • Contents amount: the total replacement cost of your belongings.
  • Personal liability limit: $1,000,000 or $2,000,000.
  • Deductible: $500, $1,000 or another amount.
  • Would you like sewer backup coverage (water backing up through drains or toilets)?
