Home insurance - what we need for your quote

  • Property address, and the closing / possession date if you are buying.
  • About the home: year built, approx. living area (sq ft), number of storeys, exterior (brick, siding, etc.), and whether the basement is finished (approx. %).
  • Updates: the year the roof, electrical, plumbing and heating were last updated, and the type of heating (gas, electric, oil, wood stove).
  • Protection: sump pump, backwater valve, monitored fire / burglar alarm.
  • Anything extra: pool or hot tub, trampoline, dogs (breed), a rented unit or basement apartment, or a business run from home.
  • Mortgage: the lender's name and address and your mortgage number, if you have a mortgage.
  • Current policy: if the home is insured now, the declarations page of your current policy (insurer, coverage amounts and renewal date).
  • Claims: any home insurance claims in the last 5 years.
  • Phone number and email address to reach you.
