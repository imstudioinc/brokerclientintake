Home insurance - what we need for your quote

  • Property address and postal code, and the closing / possession date if you are buying.
  • About each named insured: date of birth, occupation and marital status (needed for the quote and the credit-based pricing).
  • About the home: year built, approx. living area (sq ft), number of storeys, exterior (brick, siding, etc.), whether the basement is finished (approx. %), and the number of bathrooms, kitchens and bedrooms, and the garage.
  • Updates: the year the roof, electrical, plumbing and heating were last updated, and the type of each: roof covering (asphalt shingles, metal ...), wiring (copper, aluminum, knob & tube), electrical panel (breakers or fuses) and amps, plumbing pipes (copper, PEX, galvanized, Poly-B).
  • Heating: gas, electric, oil, propane, heat pump or wood. For an oil tank: inside or outside, the material and the year on its label. For a wood or pellet stove: when it was installed.
  • Protection: sump pump (with battery backup), backwater valve, automatic water shut-off, and a monitored fire / burglar alarm.
  • Anything extra: pool or hot tub, trampoline, dogs (breed), a rented unit or basement apartment, roomers, or a business run from home.
  • Mortgage: the lender's name and address, and your mortgage number, if you have a mortgage.
  • Current policy: if the home is insured now, the declarations page (insurer, policy number, coverage amounts and renewal date), and how long you have been continuously insured.
  • Claims: any home insurance claims or water damage in the last 5 years (date, what happened and the amount), and whether any insurer has cancelled or declined you.
  • Phone number and email address to reach you.

I will also need your signed consent to check your claims history (HITS) and your credit score - I will send the form.
