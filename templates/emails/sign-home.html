Thank you for choosing your home insurance with {insurer}. To set up your policy, please review and sign the attached documents:

  • Home insurance application - please check every detail (address, year built, updates, mortgage, claims history) before you sign.
  • Consent form - allows us to check your claims history (HITS) and your credit score, which the insurance company uses to price your policy.
  • PAC form (pre-authorized payments) with a void cheque or a bank direct-deposit form. If the account is a business account, please tell me.
  • Questionnaires that apply to your home (sewer backup, oil tank, wood stove or rented unit) - fully completed and signed.

If you have them, please also send:

  • The alarm certificate if you have a monitored alarm - the alarm discount is only applied once the certificate is in.
  • A letter of experience from your previous insurer, to confirm your claims-free years.
  • Photos of the home, if the insurance company asks for them.

Your policy can't be bound until we have the signed application and your payment (or the signed PAC form). You can sign electronically or print, sign and send back photos or scans.

If anything on the application is not correct, let me know before you sign.
